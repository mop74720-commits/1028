Three-subproblem organization
  P1: compatible Birkhoff form -> canonical lift -> local endpoint projection
      -> explicit generator -> weighted midpoint and ordinary midpoint ->
      noncommuting matrix implementation. The endpoint proposition is proved
      directly and explains the original Theorem A role; the midpoint and
      Central4 truncations explain Theorem B's constructive role. No numbered
      theorem from Feng's book is presented as an unchecked verbatim statement.
  P2: same known-kinetic plant, analytic potential residual, shared integral
      fit and magnetic port interface; h0, h*, hhat and hc remain distinct.
      The general CHNN decoder and larger analytic readout are retained, while
      the primary reproduced fit has one cosine coefficient plus damping.
  P3: control inside the same midpoint equation; the actual closed loop has
      fixed deltaR/deltagamma; exact versus weighted structure is separated.
      The finite-time bound includes Delta^2 + eta_Delta/Delta. Numerical
      error to the same actual closed loop differs from its model error to h0.
      The principal KAM object is the exact conservative implicit map of hc
      compared with the nominal h0 normal form. Route B is a supplement extension.

Current mathematical details
  - x=(X,Y); J=[0 I;-I 0]; N=diag(nu); h*=h0+R*; hhat=h0+Rhat;
    deltaR=R*-Rhat; hc=h0+deltaR; deltagamma=gamma-gammahat.
  - U is a dimensionless two-component generalized force. The controlled
    phase increment is Delta*(0,U) in R4. Physical active torque is tau_B.
  - Birkhoff/flow matrices are 2n, mass is n, endpoint transform is 4n.
    Projection invertibility is not invertibility of the graph gradient f_w.
  - Canonical lift permits M(q); simple weighted p updates require constant
    mass. Ordinary damped midpoint does not inherit the exponential factor.
  - Exact symplecticity is the property of an exactly defined conservative
    map. Finite solver residuals and roundoff are implementation errors.
  - The primary four control ablations use midpoint. New P1/P3 checks also
    test Central4 on the same nonlinear conservative h0; its fourth-order
    result is not transferred to general damped feedback.
  - Central4 scalar correction f^T h'' f and the full magnetic increment/Jac
    are written in ss:central4 and ss:central4-magnetic. Cross-mode terms stay.
  - eta_Delta is the residual of the increment equation, not the Newton step.
    O(Delta^3) residuals preserve order two; O(Delta^5) preserves order four
    under the separately stated smoothness and stable-branch assumptions.
  - Complex continuation uses independent zeta,chi. No holomorphic-in-zeta
    assertion for a nonconstant real energy; true and learned residuals both
    require the stated analytic continuation.
  - Quartic twist retains cross-action terms. Full R6 includes the generated
    Lie terms, not just the cosine tail. Small-divisor tests through degree
    four do not establish all-integer Diophantine conditions.
  - The actual physical-coordinate numerical map is conjugated by the
    normal-form chart; midpoint is not claimed covariant under nonlinear maps.
  - Shang's discrete path uses C epsilon + C Delta^p, tau_D>3 for n=2,
    complex domains, frequency margins and the stated smallness threshold.
    No new finite-amplitude interval KAM certificate is claimed.
  - Route-B original control/error/oracle labels and ss:tracking now live in
    extension_tracking_theory.tex. The oracle keeps the same learned reference.
    The two-route budgets and continuous theorem are also supplementary.
