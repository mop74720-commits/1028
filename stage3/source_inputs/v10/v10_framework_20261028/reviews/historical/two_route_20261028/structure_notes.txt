Shared conventions
  x=(X,Y); J=[0 I;-I 0]; mathsf N=diag(nu).
  h_0 is the nominal physical energy in balanced variables.
  h_*=h_0+R_* is the declared true conservative model.
  hhat=h_0+Rhat is the learned reference energy.
  deltaR=R_*-Rhat and deltagamma=gamma-gammahat.
  h_c=h_0+deltaR is the conservative part of Route A.
  U is dimensionless generalized force; the physical torque is tau_B.
  tau is dimensionless time; Delta is its step; Delta_t is physical step.
  General CHNN energy+dissipation decoder is retained in mechanics and connected
  to finite analytic readouts here. The rerun only fits one cosine-potential
  coefficient plus damping; the full 35-feature extension has no new training.

Principal mathematical decisions
  - Birkhoff K and flow Jacobians have size 2n, mass matrices size n; endpoint
    transformation has size 4n. No invertibility of zero-step graph derivative f_w.
  - The weighted canonical transformation allows M(q); the explicit weighted
    momentum update specializes to constant mass and constant alpha.
  - Ordinary midpoint on a damped field and weighted midpoint are distinct.
  - The exactly solved autonomous central map is exact symplectic; its primitive
    is Ybar^T deltaX - S_Delta. Finite Newton/roundoff errors are separate.
  - Central4 retained: S=Delta h + Delta^3 f^T h'' f / 24. The full derivation
    is in ss:central4. New-parameter experiments use only ordinary midpoint.
  - The complex continuation uses independent zeta,chi, with chi=conj(zeta)
    imposed only on the real physical slice. No assumption that real energy
    is holomorphic in zeta alone. General-force normalization is i f / sqrt2.
  - Four objects above remain distinct. Only exact conservative realization
    allows classical KAM for Route A's actual loop; Route B KAM is for the
    learned reference, and tracking is a separate forced stable error equation.
  - Integral Gram normalization is n L_I for momentum-only observations, n=2.
    The 35-feature full-state extension instead has 4 L_I rows and 36 unknowns.
    It cannot reuse the two-column numerical Gram bound.
  - Quartic normal form includes the cross-action entries. For cubic terms,
    Z4=<H4+{H3,W3}/2>, {H2,W3}=-H3. The cosine model has no cubic term.
  - Full R6 includes Lie terms proportional to kappa^2, not only cosine tail.
  - Flow and map Diophantine conditions are stated separately. Conjugation of
    the actual physical-coordinate map is used, not a claim of midpoint
    covariance under nonlinear canonical maps.
  - The map path is Shang 1999, Theorem 2, Remark 2 and Section 4: joint
    generator-per-step budget C epsilon + C Delta^p. For n=2 use tau_D>3;
    frequency-boundary margins and analytic domains are required.
  - No old finite-amplitude torus, interval remainder, probability, measure
    fraction, COMSOL identification or Central4 experiment is transplanted.
