Current narrative:
  The main application follows U_A = gammahat Y + grad Rhat throughout.
  It connects the same coupled nominal magnetic energy to the physical ports,
  realized-input reconstruction, the actual physical closed loop, modal
  enrichment and a separate conditional clearance argument.
  Route B is referenced only as the related tracking extension in Supplement S4.
  It is not a parallel organizing objective of the main application.

Retained physical basis:
  All pre-existing labeled mechanics equations and the complete parameter table
  are unchanged from the audited baseline. The 32-element beam includes terminal
  translation mass and rotary inertia, with centre of mass at the beam endpoint.
  Coating, adhesive and offset mounting masses are not included.
  C = alpha Mf is a declared effective damping model. The corresponding endpoint
  damping terms are not independent measurements of metal-core dissipation.
  General rod/M(q) mechanics is retained: c_geo is inertia, D_N is dissipation,
  and B_u is a physical generalized input map. A momentum readout G1 produces
  physical damping -M grad_p G1, not automatically -grad_p G1.
  The Rayleigh readout gamma v^T N^-1 v / 2 produces -gamma Y only when v = NY.

Dimensions and coordinate levels:
  New Table mech:dimension-table distinguishes the 64-by-64 full FE mass Mf,
  mass-normalized modal I_n (I2 for two modes), and balanced Meff = N^-1.
  The full FE phase space has dimension 128. Two-mode phase states and residuals
  have dimension 4; the joint endpoint transformation has dimension 8.
  For general n, phase-flow and endpoint-transformation Jacobians have sizes
  2n-by-2n and 4n-by-4n, respectively. They are not mechanical mass matrices.
  The controller's continuous generalized input U is a two-vector; F and tau_B
  are dimensional physical ports. The step-scaled four-dimensional increment
  is defined by the theory owner in ctrl:midpoint and is referenced here.

Physical interface and new closure:
  tau_B is the active transverse-field magnetic torque, with units N m.
  mu is the magnetic dipole moment, with units A m^2.
  T = E0(theta - sin(theta)) + tau_B is the total modal moment relative to the
  biased linear stiffness. The known bias remainder must be removed before
  using T to calculate the transverse field.
  app:physical-closed-loop completes the chain:
    a_ddot = -Lambda grad V0 + (alphahat - alpha) a_dot - Lambda grad deltaR,
    alphahat = omega0 gammahat.
  The true residual is evaluated by the synthetic plant or labeled truth-only
  comparator, not by the learned control law.
  app:input-reconstruction defines U_rec and e_port for the learned group.
  Calibration, angle estimation, saturation and current limits must appear in
  the applied ports and therefore in e_port when such effects are introduced.

Same-controller enrichment:
  app:enriched-plant defines a physical full residual R_N in joules and the
  N-by-2 physical input map for N = 2, 4, 8, 64.
  A matched synthetic hierarchy uses restrictions of one declared full potential
  and satisfies R_N(Ds X, 0) = E0 R*(X). This continuation is not identified from
  two-mode data alone.
  The projected actual low dynamics contain e_mag^(N) - e_R^(N) + e_port.
  e_mag^(N) is the full-angle versus two-mode nominal magnetic difference;
  e_R^(N) is the low projection of the full residual minus grad R*(X).
  For the explicit proposed synthetic full-angle extension E0 beta* g(theta_N),
  e_R^(N) = -beta* e_mag^(N), so the combined angle term is (1+beta*) e_mag^(N).
  A low-coordinate-only potential is recorded as a different isolating model.
  The theory agent independently checked this new projection and sign relation.
  The actual full endpoint angle must be used in the ideal port inverse;
  substituting an estimated two-mode angle contributes implementation error.

Validation protocol and evidence status:
  app:validation-stages restores the fixed-controller 4/8/64-mode and independent
  FE/COMSOL protocol. Only the two-mode controlled-response stage is complete.
  Higher-mode trajectories, independent FE driven comparisons, noise and
  saturation tests, hardware allocation and finite-amplitude certification
  are not reported as completed results.
  Linear 16/32/64-element frequency convergence is distinct from changing the
  number of retained modes in a controlled-response test.
  The proposed tests keep the fitted law, low initial state, nominal reference
  and port convention fixed, introduce declared high-mode initial conditions,
  and record the full centreline, slopes, modal response and magnetic demands.
  The two-mode truth-only comparator remains separate from full-state exact
  feedback; it does not silently cancel high-mode defects.

Energy and clearance:
  The Cauchy-Schwarz displacement bound holds for any clamped H2 shape, including
  all retained modes. The static tip-force shape is only its equality case.
  A complete nominal energy has nonnegative bending, bias and kinetic terms.
  A full residual contributes -a_dot^T grad R_N to the nominal-energy derivative;
  that power needs a bound, or an augmented-energy lower bound must be proved.
  The reported compensation initial nominal energy is 12.656720734508879 nJ,
  above the selected 0.95 Ec cap of 9.518731216065792 nJ. No work governor is
  implemented in the reported runs, so sampled displacement maxima are not
  consequences of that energy-cap theorem.
  The tracking initial state and its distinct energy belong to Supplement S4.
  Sensing/execution and sample-and-hold results remain conditional algebraic
  bounds without invented hardware tolerances.
